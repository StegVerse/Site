## Preserved normative posture

The received specification defines the ITC as the minimum interoperable handoff between discovery and downstream coordination, governance, or execution. It requires preservation of interpreted intent, criteria, constraints, recommendation, reasoning, evidence references, confidence, uncertainties, unresolved dependencies, and provenance.

The specification expressly excludes consent, authority, admissibility, commitment, execution state, outcome state, legal decisions, and governance decisions. It defines both over-inclusion and under-inclusion as boundary failures.
